# Nurture email drafts

These are drafts for a 3-email nurture sequence. They are not wired up in this build (the free Mailchimp plan limits automations). Use `*|FNAME|*` as the first-name merge tag.

## Email 1: welcome (on signup)
**Subject:** Welcome, here's what to expect

Hi *|FNAME|*,

Thanks for signing up. Over the next few days I'll send two short emails about marketing automation: one practical tip and one idea you can try this week.

To make sure they reach you, reply to this email with one marketing process you wish ran by itself. I read every reply.

Talk soon,
Tabitha

## Email 2: a useful tip (2 days later)
**Subject:** 3 checks before you automate anything

Hi *|FNAME|*,

Most automations fail for boring reasons, not technical ones. Before you build anything, check three things:

1. **Is the manual process written down?** If you can't list the steps, you can't automate them.
2. **What happens when data is missing?** A blank field should go to a fix-it list, not break the flow.
3. **Who gets told when it fails?** An automation nobody monitors will quietly stop working.

Try these on one process this week.

Tabitha

## Email 3: soft call to action (5 days later)
**Subject:** One idea to try this week

Hi *|FNAME|*,

Here's a small project: pick one form on your website and trace what happens after someone submits it. Where does the data go? Who is told? How long until the person hears back?

Most teams find at least one gap in 15 minutes.

Tabitha
