# Example: HubSpot's active Meta ads

Source: Meta Ad Library, active ads matching "HubSpot", United States, viewed 31 October 2026. I paraphrase the ads rather than copy them. Ad IDs are in the swipe file.

## What I saw
| Group | Started | What the ad does | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product ads: Smart CRM, Breeze AI assistant, "your data, connected" | Sep to Oct 2026 | Short videos (15 to 33 seconds) that lead with one benefit: less admin, better AI output, connected data | Learn more |
| Feature ad: Google Ads inside HubSpot | 14 Oct 2026 | Handles the "this will be hard to set up" objection with "minutes, not months" | Get started today |
| Free resources: Instagram guide, 1,000+ AI prompts, content calendar template | 22 Jun 2026 | Offers a free download tied to a current topic; several have multiple creative versions | Download |
| HubSpot Academy: free certification courses | Jan 2026 and still running | Free training as a long-term audience builder | Learn more |

## Patterns
1. **Two jobs, two CTAs.** Product ads say "Learn more". Free-resource ads say "Download". The offer is matched to the funnel stage.
2. **Long-running free resources.** The gated guides and templates have run since June and still run, which suggests the lead magnets are paying for themselves. Product videos are newer, so they look like tests.
3. **Each ad makes one promise.** Every ad has one benefit and one action.
4. **Multiple versions on the resource ads.** The library marks several of them as having multiple versions, which suggests ongoing creative testing.

## What a small SaaS could take from it
- Run one free resource per topic you want to be known for, and keep it running instead of replacing it every week.
- Use a different CTA for cold and warm audiences.
- Write one ad that answers your buyers' biggest objection (for example "setup is hard") and test it against a benefit ad.

## Test idea
Run two ads to the same audience: A leads with a benefit, B leads with the objection. Send both to the same page and compare the click-to-lead rate. See [ad-landing-page-match](../ad-landing-page-match/) for how to set up the page and the test.
