# Competitor Ad Research

A free, repeatable way to see what your competitors are advertising, which ads they keep running, and what you can learn from them. Use it to feed your own ad and landing page ideas. Learn the pattern, never copy the creative.

## Who it is for
Small SaaS marketing teams that have no budget for ad-spy tools.

## The free sources
| Library | What it shows | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta Ad Library | Active Facebook and Instagram ads, start date, platforms, copy, CTA | https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/ |
| Google Ads Transparency Center | Search, YouTube and display ads by advertiser or domain | https://adstransparency.google.com/ |
| LinkedIn Ad Library | Active LinkedIn ads by company or keyword | https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library |

## The method (20 minutes per competitor)
1. Search the competitor name in each library. Filter to active ads in your target country.
2. For each ad, log it in the swipe file (`swipe-file-template.csv`): hook, offer, CTA, format, start date, landing page.
3. Use run time as the winner signal. An ad that has run for months is probably paying for itself. An ad started last week is a test.
4. Group by pattern: what offers repeat, what hooks repeat, which funnel stage each ad targets.
5. Write three takeaways and one test idea for your own ads (see the example analysis).

## Example
[`example-hubspot-meta-ads.md`](example-hubspot-meta-ads.md) is a real analysis of HubSpot's active Meta ads, taken on 31 October 2026.

## Limits
- The libraries show what runs, not how well it performs. They have no spend or conversion data for normal ads, so run time is only a proxy.
- Copy ideas and angles, not wording or images. Other people's ads are their copyright.
- The worked example uses the Meta Ad Library only. The Google and LinkedIn steps follow the same method but are not demonstrated here.
